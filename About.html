About Cha Ride – ESSU Campus Transport

About Cha Ride

Cha Ride is a campus transportation system designed to help students, staff, and teachers move around the ESSU campus safely and conveniently. It connects passengers with available tricycle and motorcycle drivers, making campus travel faster and more organized.

The system keeps track of all passengers, drivers, vehicles, rides, and payments. Passengers can easily request rides, while drivers can accept and complete ride requests efficiently. Payments are recorded in the system, ensuring that transactions are transparent and reliable.

Admin staff manage the overall operation of Cha Ride. They monitor driver availability, track rides, and handle fare management. This ensures that the system runs smoothly and that both passengers and drivers have a positive experience.

Cha Ride is simple, efficient, and reliable. It is designed to make commuting on campus easy, safe, and affordable while helping the university keep transportation organized and well-managed.
