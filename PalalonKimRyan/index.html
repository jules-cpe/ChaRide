About CHARIDE

Cha Ride is a campus-based transportation service created to provide students, faculty, and staff of ESSU with a safe and convenient way to travel within the campus. Our riders undergo proper training and follow strict safety standards. We aim to make daily commuting easier, faster, and more affordable for everyone.
